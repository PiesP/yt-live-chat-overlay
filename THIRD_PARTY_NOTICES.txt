Unicode Character Database
https://www.unicode.org/Public/13.0.0/ucd/ReadMe.txt

bidi-js contains generated Unicode bidirectional character, bracket and mirroring
tables. Its generator selects UCD 13.0.0 for DerivedBidiClass.txt, BidiBrackets.txt
and BidiMirroring.txt, and uses UnicodeData.txt for canonical bracket mappings.
The published tables can be reproduced with the Unicode 13.0.0 data files.
